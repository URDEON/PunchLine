# Servo: Robots and Mechs Sound Effects: FREE DEMO

**23 of the 106 sounds from *Servo: Robots and Mechs Sound Effects*, free to use.**

This is a real working subset, not a teaser reel. Everything here is the same audio as the full pack:
23 sounds in both `.wav` and `.ogg`, under the same licence.

## What is in the demo, and why

The point of this pack is a robot body that changes in step with your game. So the demo is chosen to let
you judge exactly that:

- **the whole stride ladder** (5 rungs, one take each): does each footstep land heavier than the last?
- **the whole power ladder** (5 seamless loops): the core brightening as power rises
- **the whole alert ladder** (5 seamless loops): swap them as the threat rises and the beeps come sooner
- **the shortest and the longest malfunction**: a scratch and a breakdown
- **every take of the servo sweep**: play them at random and the same arm never moves the same way twice
- **one target lock, one armour hit and the assembly line loop**

11 of the demo files are seamless loops with loop points written into the file.
