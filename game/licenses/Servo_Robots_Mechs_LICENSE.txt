================================================================================
PROVENANCE — WHY THIS LICENCE CAN BE THIS SIMPLE
================================================================================

Every sound in this pack was synthesised from scratch in code. There are no
recorded samples, no third-party source material, no stock-library content, and
nothing derived from another sound pack. The synthesis is deterministic: the
same code run twice produces identical audio.

That means there is no upstream licence sitting behind this one, no chain of
rights that could be revoked, and no third-party claim that could ever surface
against your project. It is also why the terms above can be short: we are not
passing along restrictions imposed on us, because there are none.

AI DISCLOSURE, stated truthfully: the CODE that generates these sounds was
written with AI assistance. The SOUNDS are self-contained procedural synthesis —
oscillators, filters, modal resonators and envelopes evaluated in code — and no
generative audio model was used at any point in producing them. The cover art
and store graphics are AI-generated.
